# Echo Dungeon V14 - Blind First Edition

A voice-controlled dungeon crawler RPG designed specifically for blind and visually impaired players.

**Making accessibility the norm in blind gaming.**

## Blind First. Always.

This game was built from the ground up for blind players:
- **Tap anywhere on the screen** to speak a command
- **Everything is communicated through speech** - polite, cheery, and clear
- **No visual elements** - no buttons to find, no text to read
- **No advantage to sighted players** - the experience is identical for everyone

## Features

- **100% Voice Controlled**: Tap anywhere, speak your command
- **Full Audio Experience**: All game information delivered through speech
- **Three Classes**: Warrior, Mage, and Rogue with unique abilities
- **Five Races**: Human, Elf, Dwarf, Orc, and Halfling with attribute bonuses
- **Attribute System**: Strength, Dexterity, and Wisdom shape your character
- **Deep Progression**: Infinite dungeon floors with scaling difficulty
- **Named Save System**: Save and load characters by name
- **Distance-Based Dungeon**: Enemies get harder as you approach the boss
- **Inventory Management**: Auto-sorting junk bag for easy selling

## Quick Start

1. Open the game in Chrome or Edge (speech recognition required)
2. Host via HTTPS (required for microphone access) or use GitHub Pages
3. Tap anywhere on the screen
4. Say "new game" to begin
5. Follow the voice prompts to create your character
6. Adventure awaits!

## Voice Commands

### Movement
- **North** - Move north
- **South** - Move south
- **East** - Move east
- **West** - Move west

### Exploration
- **Look around** - Describe current room
- **Open chest** - Loot treasure chests
- **Drink fountain** - Use healing fountain
- **Use shrine** - Receive a blessing
- **Use stairs** - Descend to next floor
- **Merchant** - Talk to merchants

### Combat
- **Attack** - Basic attack
- **Defend** - Reduce damage by 50% and drink a potion
- **Special** - Use your class ability
- **Cast [spell name]** - Use a learned spell
- **Flee** - Attempt to escape

### During Defend
- **Health potion** - Drink a health potion
- **Mana potion** - Drink a mana potion
- **Brace** - Skip potion, continue defending

### Inventory
- **Inventory** - List your items
- **Junk bag** - List items to sell
- **Equip [item]** - Equip a weapon or armor
- **Remove [slot]** - Unequip from weapon, armor, ring, etc.
- **Inspect [item]** - Hear item details
- **Read book** - Learn ability from a book

### Merchant
- **What do you have** - Browse merchant stock
- **Purchase [item]** - Buy an item
- **Purchase 3 [item]** - Buy multiple items
- **Sell junk** - Sell everything in your junk bag
- **Leave** - Exit the merchant

### Character
- **Status** - Full character information
- **Attributes** - View strength, dexterity, wisdom
- **Strength** - Spend point on strength (when available)
- **Dexterity** - Spend point on dexterity (when available)
- **Wisdom** - Spend point on wisdom (when available)

### System
- **Save** - Save your character
- **Load [name]** - Load a character
- **List saves** - Show saved characters
- **Help** - List commands
- **Tips** - Get a helpful hint
- **Readme** - Hear game instructions
- **License** - Copyright information

## Classes

### Warrior
- High health and strength
- Uses shields for extra defense
- Special: Shield Bash (damage + stun)

### Mage
- High mana and wisdom
- Powerful spells scale with wisdom
- Special: Fireball (high damage)

### Rogue
- Balanced stats with high dexterity
- Critical hits and dodge chance
- Special: Backstab (no enemy retaliation)

## Races

- **Human**: Balanced, no bonuses or penalties
- **Elf**: +2 Dexterity, +1 Wisdom, -1 Strength
- **Dwarf**: +3 Strength, -1 Dexterity
- **Orc**: +4 Strength, -2 Wisdom
- **Halfling**: +3 Dexterity, +1 Wisdom, -2 Strength, bonus dodge

## Attributes

- **Strength**: Increases max health and physical damage
- **Dexterity**: Increases critical hit and dodge chance
- **Wisdom**: Increases max mana, spell damage, and reduces mana costs

You gain 3 attribute points each time you level up.

## The Dungeon

- Start at the center (position 6,6)
- Boss waits at the far corner (position 11,11)
- Enemies get harder as you move away from center
- Stairs near the boss lead to the next floor
- Every 10 floors, your power increases dramatically

## Browser Requirements

- Chrome 33+ or Edge 79+ (recommended)
- HTTPS connection (required for speech recognition)
- Microphone access must be allowed

## Hosting

The game requires HTTPS for speech recognition to work. Options:
- GitHub Pages (free, recommended)
- Any HTTPS web server
- Local server with SSL certificate
